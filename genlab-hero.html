About Charaka

CHARAKA is designed to enable

CHARAKA is a Government of Karnataka initiative being established as a translational platform for healthcare innovation. It brings together clinicians, engineers, researchers, startups, and policymakers to accelerate the journey from idea to adoption.

The Centre of Excellence (CoE) in MedTech, HealthTech & Assistive Tech, a section 8 not-for-profit established by the Government of Karnataka, represents a strategic public investment to translate clinical needs and scientific innovation into scalable, affordable, accessible and deployable technologies.

Our Vision

To position Karnataka as the national leader in MedTech, HealthTech and Assistive Tech innovation through translational research, advanced prototyping, startup acceleration, and public system integration.

Our Mission

To build an integrated, sustainable ecosystem that enables design, development, validation, manufacturing, and adoption of affordable and quality-competitive solutions in MedTech, HealthTech and Assistive Tech.
